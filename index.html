Bite Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Bite Rush (“the game”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Byterush, [Canada]. Contact: millionairehasib1234@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game has no account of its own. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or exact location.
  • Your progress (scores, stars, unlocked stages, collectibles, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads use your device’s advertising ID and may be personalised only if you consent.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics are collected only if you say yes when asked. You can switch this off at any time in the game settings (“Usage stats”).

Consent for ads (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

On first launch in these regions, a consent form provided by Google appears before any ad loads. You can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice later with the “Ad privacy” button in the game settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded and other ads

Some rewards (for example a revive or doubled footballs) are offered in exchange for watching an ad. Watching is always optional. Full-screen ads appear only between runs, never during gameplay.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data, if you opted in, is kept by the analytics provider for up to 14 months. To ask for deletion of analytics data, email us; you can also turn off “Usage stats” in settings, which stops collection immediately.

Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your information. Email us to exercise them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the effective date above.
